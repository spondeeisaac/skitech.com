WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Review before launch: This is a working notice for the SKITECH website. The business owner should review it for accuracy and confirm data retention, access, and applicable requirements before collecting real enquiries.

Information you submit

The project enquiry form may ask for your name, email address, organisation, service of interest, message and consent to be contacted. Please do not submit passwords, bank details, confidential financial documents, or sensitive personal information.

Why information is used

SKITECH intends to use the information you submit only to review your enquiry, contact you about your request, and manage the follow-up related to that enquiry.

How the form is processed

When configured, the website form sends your submission to a Google Apps Script web app operated for SKITECH and records enquiries in a private Google Sheet. The site owner is responsible for securing access to that sheet and configuring any notifications. The form must not be treated as operational until this connection has been tested.

Storage, access and retention

Only people authorised by SKITECH should access enquiries. SKITECH should establish and document a retention period and securely remove records that are no longer needed. The owner should verify the final arrangements before launch.

Your choices and questions

You may choose not to submit the form. For a question about an enquiry you have already submitted, contact SKITECH by phone at +233 24 968 4667 and refer to the enquiry date and service where possible.
